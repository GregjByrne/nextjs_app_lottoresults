Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 16 September 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how this website ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, processes, and protects your personal information when you visit our website, use our services, or interact with us via our contact form.

We process your data in compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK Data Protection Act, and the UK GDPR. If you do not agree with any terms in this policy, please discontinue use of the website immediately.

1. Important Regulatory Information & Data Controller

Under the EU/UK GDPR, this website operates as a Data Controller. Because we value your privacy, we have structured this policy to clearly communicate our data handling workflows transparently, without heavy legal jargon.

  • Inquiries: For any data-related queries, exercises of user rights, or privacy questions, you can reach out directly via our website’s Contact Form.

2. Information We Collect and How We Collect It

We strictly limit data collection to the minimum information necessary to provide a secure and functional experience. We collect data via two methods:

A. Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Contact Form Submissions: When you message us, we collect your Name and Email Address, alongside any personal details you freely type into the message field.

B. Information Collected Automatically

As you navigate our website, we automatically collect tracking and environmental data:

  • Technical & Infrastructure Identifiers: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type/version, device type, and operating system.
  • Behavioral & Interaction Logs: Pages viewed, timestamps, bounce rates, and navigation paths captured via server logs, pixel tags, and tracking files.

3. Lawful Bases and Purposes for Processing Data

Under Article 6 of the GDPR, we must have a specific "lawful basis" to process your information. We rely on the following grounds:

Data Category Purpose of Processing Lawful Basis (GDPR / UK GDPR)
Contact Form Data To reply to your inquiries, deliver requested services, or provide user support. Performance of a Contract / Pre-contractual steps (acting upon your direct request).
IP Address & Technical Logs To optimize page loading speeds, track site metrics, detect system abuse, or block suspicious users. Legitimate Interests (maintaining network security, site stability, and proper operations).
Cookies & Pixel Tags To measure platform traffic, personalize content, and display compliant advertisements. Consent (provided by you via our cookie acceptance mechanism).

4. Third-Party Relationships & Advertising

To fund our platform operations, we participate in selective advertising and monetization structures. We strictly control our third-party data exposures:

  • Google AdSense: We serve third-party ads managed through Google. Please note that Google uses automated identifiers and IP addresses for ad profiling and measurement. To maintain the integrity of our site, we strictly enforce rules blocking sensitive ad categories, such as those targeting race, religion, or sexual orientation.
  • Affiliate Partnerships: This site utilizes trusted affiliate platforms. If you click an external affiliate link and complete a purchase, we may receive a commission. This arrangement incurs no extra cost to you.
  • No Selling of Data: We do not trade, rent, or sell user personal data to any external marketing firms or brokers.

5. Data Retention Timelines

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the specific purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Contact Form Messages: Retained for up to 24 months following your last interaction to ensure thorough customer service, unless you request earlier deletion.
  • Server Security Logs: Automatically overwritten or purged within 90 days, unless flagged for a security review.
  • Tracking & Analytics Identifiers: Set to expire in accordance with Google AdSense standards or standard browser session limits.

6. International Data Transfers

Our website assets and content are hosted via Amazon Web Services (AWS) servers. Because AWS utilizes global cloud networks, data from EU and UK residents may be transferred to or processed in regions outside the European Economic Area (EEA) or the UK (such as the United States).

To protect your data during these transfers, our cloud framework relies on verified legal safety measures, including the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and relevant UK International Data Transfer Agreements.

7. Your Statutory Privacy Rights (EU & UK Residents)

If you reside within the EU, EEA, or the UK, you possess specific legal rights over your personal data under the GDPR:

  • Right of Access: You can request a clear copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request that we amend or correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can demand that we delete your contact form submissions or logs, subject to legal overrides.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You can object to our processing of your data based on legitimate business interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where data processing is based on your explicit consent (e.g., cookies), you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time.

To enforce any of these rights, please message us directly through our Contact Form. We will review and respond to your request within the statutory one-month timeframe.

8. Cookie Management and Opt-Out Controls

While cookies are essential for an optimized browsing experience, you maintain total control over how they are stored on your machine. You can block, restrict, or clear cookies directly inside your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Enter chrome://settings/cookies into your address bar to adjust permissions or wipe site data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Enter edge://settings/privacy into your address bar to fine-tune your tracking protections and remove cookies.
  • Global Opt-Out: If you comprehensively disagree with our structural data processing methods, your ultimate recourse is to terminate your session and cease using the website.

9. Regulatory Complaints

If you believe our data processing methods infringe upon your legal rights under the GDPR, you have a statutory right to lodge an official complaint with a supervisory authority:

  • UK Residents: You can file a report directly with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
  • EU Residents: You can contact the local Data Protection Authority (DPA) inside your respective EU Member State.

10. Modifications to This Policy

We reserve the right to revise this Privacy Policy at any time to adapt to evolving technical frameworks, site features, or statutory revisions. Updates will be published seamlessly to this exact URL with a revised "Effective Date" posted at the top.
